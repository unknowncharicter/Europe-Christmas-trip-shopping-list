    Good to know

    • Leather sneakers are not waterproofSnow will soak them. Look for waterproof kids boots, or buy a waterproofing spray for the sneakers. Kathmandu and Macpac may have kids snow boots, so check on Level 4.
    • SizesElliot is a Small. Some shops call that adult S, others boys 14, so compare. Clara is 6–8.
    • Dad's shoes: check stock firstCheck the JD Sports website or call the shop to make sure they have the grey Handball Spezial in US 9.5.
    • Still need for the snow?Beanie, gloves, thermal socks and a neck warmer. All are at Kathmandu and Macpac on Level 4. Tap "Add item" to add them.
